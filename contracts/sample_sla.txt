SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") is entered into between:

  CloudServices GmbH, a German company ("Provider")
  TechCorp UK Ltd, a United Kingdom company ("Customer")

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. SERVICES
   Provider agrees to supply the following services to Customer:
   - Cloud infrastructure hosting on AWS EU-WEST-1
   - 24/7 monitoring and alerting
   - Technical support via email and phone (business hours)
   - Monthly performance reports

2. UPTIME GUARANTEE
   Provider guarantees 99.5% uptime measured monthly, excluding:
   - Scheduled maintenance windows (announced 72 hours in advance)
   - Force majeure events
   - Customer-caused incidents

3. RESPONSE TIMES
   - P1 Critical: Response within 15 minutes, resolution within 4 hours
   - P2 High: Response within 1 hour, resolution within 8 hours
   - P3 Medium: Response within 4 hours, resolution within 2 business days
   - P4 Low: Response within 1 business day

4. PENALTIES
   For each percentage point of uptime below 99.5%, Customer receives a service
   credit of 5% of the monthly fee, up to a maximum of 30%.

5. LIABILITY CAP
   Provider's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid
   by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

6. GOVERNING LAW
   This SLA shall be governed by German law. Jurisdiction: Berlin, Germany.

7. TERM
   Initial term: 12 months from Effective Date, with automatic renewal unless
   terminated with 60 days written notice.
